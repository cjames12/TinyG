# Product Brief: TinyG

## Executive Summary

TinyG is a touchscreen game for young children (ages 3-6) that teaches shape recognition through playful matching. A child sees 6-8 colorful shapes on the left and their silhouettes on the right; tap a shape, tap the matching silhouette, and watch it snap into place with rewarding feedback. It's designed to be fun first, educational second—a tool for early learning that feels like play.

## The Problem

Three-year-olds are naturally curious about shapes, colors, and cause-and-effect. Parents want tools that feel safe, hold attention, and support brain development without screen-time guilt. Existing shape games often feel too mechanical or adult-designed; TinyG meets kids where they are: with instant, joyful feedback and interactions sized for tiny hands.

## The Solution

**Phase 1 (Core — now shipping):**
- **Shape matching loop:** Tap a shape to select it (with clear visual cue), then tap a silhouette to match. On success, the shape locks in place and a success sound plays. On mismatch, light vibration + sound, and the shape returns to start so they can try again.
- **Variable difficulty:** Each round displays 6-8 random shapes (not fixed 5). Round ends when all shapes are matched. Next round shuffles 6-8 fresh shapes.
- **Visual feedback on match:** When a shape is successfully matched, the original shape location fades/clears, so the child sees cause-and-effect: "I moved this, it's gone from there."
- **Platform:** Tablet and phone, touch-optimized. 48 CSS-pixel minimum targets for fat-finger taps.
- **Layout:** Shapes and silhouettes are scattered randomly across the screen (not left/right panes). Creates a more dynamic, exploratory experience.

**Phase 2 (Delight — to follow):**
- **Celebration animation:** Sparkling or confetti particle effects when a shape snaps into place, making the moment feel triumphant.
- **Character traits:** Shapes evolve beyond flat silhouettes—cute, emoji-like designs with eyes, blinking, and facial expressions. Shapes feel alive, respond to the child's actions.

## Who This Serves

**Primary user:** A child aged 3-6 who is learning shapes, enjoys tapping and immediate feedback, and benefits from visual-spatial play.

**Secondary users:** Parents who want developmentally appropriate screen time—a tool that feels intentional, not passive.

## Success Criteria

- **Phase 1:** Game is playable end-to-end. Child can match all shapes in a round within 3-5 minutes. Mismatch feedback (vibration + sound) is distinguishable from success. Visual fading of matched shapes is clear.
- **Phase 2:** When activated, celebration effects add visible joy; child shows delight (engagement, smiling, repeat play). Animated character shapes don't degrade performance or accessibility.

## Scope

**In for Phase 1:**
- 6-8 random shapes per round (not fixed 5)
- Tap-to-select, tap-to-match interaction
- Visual selection cue (exact animation TBD)
- Success sound + locked shape in place
- Mismatch vibration + sound + shape returns to start
- Faded/cleared origin shape after successful match
- Round replay after all matches
- Accessible touch targets (48px minimum)

**Out for Phase 1 (Phase 2 or later):**
- Celebration animations (sparkling/confetti)
- Animated character shapes (emoji-like, eyes, expressions)
- Multiple game modes (puzzles, coloring)
- Difficulty progression
- Parent controls or analytics

## Vision

TinyG starts as a shape sorter, but the vision is a **collection of playful learning games for ages 3-6**, each focused on one core concept (shapes, colors, counting, patterns). Over time, as the child grows, TinyG grows with them—from simple matching to richer puzzles and creative play. The underlying principle stays constant: instant joy, clear cause-and-effect, no frustration.
